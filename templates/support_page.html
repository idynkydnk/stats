Support

Need help with Beach Volleyball Statistics? Contact {{ support_email }}. Include the app version, what you were trying to do, and the error message. Never send passwords or sign-in codes.

Signing in

You can browse public stats without signing in. Sign in to save your own games and manage your data. If an email code does not arrive, check spam, confirm the address, and request a fresh code. Apple users can use Hide My Email. A temporary connection error can be retried without creating another account.

AI permission and uploads

AI generation and automated content checks need your permission before information is sent to OpenAI. Manage this in More → Privacy and safety in the app or Privacy and safety settings on the website. If a safety check is unavailable, try later; content is not accepted without a successful check.

Report content or block an author

Use Report on a recap or flyer to send it to the administrator for review. Signed-in users can also block an author and manage their blocked list in Privacy and safety settings. If you cannot access the report control, email us the content link and a short explanation. Reports are reviewed so inappropriate content can be removed and abusive accounts restricted.

Delete your account

In the iPhone app, open More → Delete account. This removes your personal account and its owned data. For an older Apple account, the app may explain how to disconnect Apple access manually after deletion. If an operation is still running, wait for it to finish and retry. For a request involving someone else's records or a problem accessing your account, contact us.

Connection and saved content

Internet access is required for sign-in, syncing, and AI features. If a save is interrupted, check your game history before adding the same game again. AI work can take time; check its progress before starting another request.
