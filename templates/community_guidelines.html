Community guidelines

Keep Stats useful and welcoming for the people you play with. These rules cover player names, photos, comments, prompts, recaps, flyers, and generated pictures.

  • Do not post harassment, threats, hateful attacks, sexual exploitation, nonconsensual intimate imagery, or graphic violence.
  • Do not impersonate people, disclose private information, or use someone's photo without permission. Get the necessary parent or guardian permission before including a child.
  • Use only content you own or have permission to share. Do not upload stolen images or misleading claims about other people.
  • Keep jokes friendly. AI output can be inaccurate or offensive; review it before sharing and correct or remove problems.
  • Do not evade moderation, create replacement accounts to avoid a suspension, or submit false reports to harass others.

Reporting and enforcement

Use Report on the relevant recap or flyer. You can also contact {{ support_email }} with a link and explanation. The administrator reviews reports, removes inappropriate content, and can restrict abusive accounts. Automated checks can reject content before publication but cannot detect every problem.

Signed-in users can block an author to hide that author's content in Stats and unblock them in Privacy and safety settings. Blocking does not revoke links or copies already shared outside Stats.

For a moderation mistake or appeal, contact support with the content link. Do not re-upload removed material while it is being reviewed.
