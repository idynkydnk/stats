Privacy policy

Effective October 21, 2026

Beach Volleyball Statistics ("Stats") provides game tracking, player statistics, and optional AI recaps and pictures through its iPhone app and website. For privacy questions, contact the developer at {{ support_email }}.

Information we handle

  • Account information: your name, email address, account identifier, sign-in provider identifier, and the credentials or tokens needed to authenticate you. Apple and Google handle their own sign-in credentials; we do not receive their passwords.
  • Games and player profiles: player names, nicknames, scores, dates, game locations you enter, comments, and optional profile details such as height, birth date, email, and character descriptions.
  • Content: photos you select, prompts, generated images, recaps, flyers, and your edits. Only upload information and pictures you have permission to use and share.
  • Service and safety information: sign-in and activity records, network information in hosting/security logs, consent choices, content reports, and blocked-account preferences.

Face ID and Touch ID authentication take place on your device. Stats does not receive your biometric measurements. Selecting a face photo to upload is a separate, optional action. Stats does not use device location services to track you; game locations are information people enter.

Why we use it

We use this information to sign you in, save and calculate statistics, provide requested content, deliver sign-in or subscribed emails, maintain security, respond to support requests, and moderate abuse. We do not sell your personal information, display third-party advertising, or track you across other companies' apps and websites for advertising.

Who can see your content

Personal accounts keep their own game records. The Stats to include controls determine what you browse, and sharing controls determine when other permitted accounts can include your stats. KT Stats is a public dataset. Recaps and flyers have shareable links: people who receive a link may view or save the content. Do not put confidential information in a recap, flyer, or shared player profile. Blocking an author changes what you see in Stats; it cannot remove copies someone has already saved or shared elsewhere.

Hosting, sign-in, and email

The website and account records are hosted through PythonAnywhere. Apple or Google process sign-in when you choose their sign-in buttons. Our configured email service delivers verification codes and emails you request or subscribe to. These providers process the information needed to provide their services. Service providers may process information outside your country.

Optional AI and content safety checks

AI sharing is on by default; there is no separate opt-in step. When you use AI generation or submit content for automated safety checks, OpenAI receives the selected player names, game results, dates and locations, comments, prompts, descriptions, and selected player/reference photos needed to create recaps and pictures. OpenAI also checks submitted text and images for objectionable content. Generated text and images may be checked before publication.

These transfers are for generation and content safety. We use OpenAI's API services rather than a public chat account. The provider may retain service or abuse-monitoring records under its applicable terms; this is not a promise of zero retention. See OpenAI's API data controls. This version does not send content to Google Gemini for generation.

You can turn AI sharing off in Privacy and safety settings on the website or More → Privacy and safety in the app. Browsing and ordinary game tracking remain available. Turning sharing off stops future covered requests; it cannot recall information already sent for a completed or in-progress request. You must have the affected people's permission before submitting their information or photos.

Retention and deletion

We retain account records and saved content while needed to provide the service. In the app, More → Delete account lets a personal-account owner delete their account and owned games, player records, uploads, recaps, and flyers. Account deletion also removes sign-in access and revokes associated Apple sign-in authorization when a usable token is available. For an older Apple account without a usable token, deletion still removes the account and the app explains how to disconnect Apple access manually. You may need to retry after an active operation finishes or a temporary service problem is resolved.

Deletion does not erase independently entered records owned by other accounts or copies other people have saved. Contact us to request correction or removal of personal information in someone else's records. A one-way reservation of the former login name remains to prevent old sessions from accessing a new account with the same login name. Hosting logs and restricted backups may persist under their retention cycles; restored backups must have deletions reapplied before being used to serve data.

Your choices and contact

You can browse public stats without an account, choose what photos to upload, edit your records, turn off AI sharing, block authors, and report content. Email subscriptions can be changed with their unsubscribe links. To request access, correction, or removal of information you cannot manage yourself, email {{ support_email }}. Do not send passwords, sign-in codes, or private keys.

Stats is not directed to children under 13. Do not submit a child's personal information or photo without the necessary parent or guardian permission. Contact us if you believe a child has provided information without that permission.

We will update this page when our practices change.
